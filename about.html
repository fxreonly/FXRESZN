FXRE

FXRE (pronounced Fire) is a genre-blending artist and producer from Lagos, Nigeria. Fusing elements of Afrobeats, Afro-house, alternative music, rage and melodic rap, FXRE creates a sound that moves between infectious rhythms, emotional melodies and cinematic storytelling.

Driven by an instinct for experimentation and a passion for sonic world-building, FXRE approaches music as more than entertainment. His artistry reflects ambition, personal evolution, confidence, faith, nightlife and the complexities of navigating life.

As an independent artist and producer, FXRE takes a hands-on approach to his craft, contributing to the production, recording, mixing and mastering of his music.

His vision is to build a distinctive musical identity and a lasting creative universe.
